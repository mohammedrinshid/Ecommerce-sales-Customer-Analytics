# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **E-Commerce Sales and Customer data** to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, regional performance, and profitability.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, starting from raw data preparation and cleaning to SQL analysis and interactive visualization using Power BI.

The main objective is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful **business insights and actionable recommendations** that can support better decision-making.

---

## Business Objectives

The analysis aims to answer key business questions such as:

* What is the overall sales performance?
* How are revenue and profit performing?
* What are the monthly/periodic sales and profit trends?
* Which products are performing the best and worst?
* Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
* Who are the most valuable customers?
* How do discounts affect sales and profitability?
* Which areas require improvement?
* What actions can management take to improve business performance?
